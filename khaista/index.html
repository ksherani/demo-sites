BEFORE WE MEET

The little
details.

A good beauty day starts
with feeling prepared.

How do I book an appointment?

Send Khaista a WhatsApp message with your preferred date, service and location. She can confirm availability, your quote and any booking terms before you commit. An enquiry does not reserve an appointment.

Where are appointments available?

Khaista is based in Pontprennau, Cardiff. Ask about the current appointment location and whether travel is available for your date before making plans.

Can I bring inspiration for my look?

Absolutely — share your favourite looks, outfit colours and the level of glam you enjoy. Inspiration is a starting point for discussing a look that suits you.

What should I mention before my appointment?

Please tell Khaista about any allergies or skin sensitivities before booking. You can also discuss preparation, products and any questions specific to your appointment directly with her.

Do you offer other beauty treatments?

Khaista has also advertised facials, waxing and brow services. Message her to confirm which treatments are currently available and to discuss suitability and prices.

Are these photos Khaista’s work?

The photographs on this concept website are editorial inspiration, not client results or photographs of Khaista. Ask her directly for examples of her own work.
